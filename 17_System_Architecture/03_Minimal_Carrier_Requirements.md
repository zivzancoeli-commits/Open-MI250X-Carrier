## Design Risks

| Label | Risk | Impact | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verified | The project rules require unknowns to be documented rather than guessed. | Any schematic made from assumptions would violate the project methodology. | `AI_PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`; `AI_TASKS.md`; `README.md` |
| Unknown | OAM connector details are missing. | Incorrect pin assignment could damage the MI250X module or carrier. | `15_Reverse_Engineering/01_OAM_Pin_Mapping.md`; `Wanted_Documents.md` |
