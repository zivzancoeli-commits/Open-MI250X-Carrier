TrailFeatures June 2, 2026, 5:43am 20

I think they were more sharing that in the past the SXM standard was also something that was once an enterprise server option only. Then one day V100s got cheap enough that someone put in the effort to adapt them to PCIe cards. Now it’s trivial to toss them into a gaming system to run local models.

OAM is indeed a different standard. But in some ways it’s a better one since it’s more open-source. Once someone puts the effort into figure out the power and bootstrap blockers, there is little to no red-tape to open the floodgates on OAM boards.
